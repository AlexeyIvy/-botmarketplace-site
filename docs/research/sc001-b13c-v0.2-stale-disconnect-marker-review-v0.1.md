# SC001 — B13-C Collector v0.2 Stale Disconnect Marker Review v0.1

Date: 2026-09-19
Status: **ENGINEERING REVIEW / PRE-SYSTEMD MIGRATION**

## Issue

The collector persists last_disconnect_ms after a successful reconnect.

On a later process restart this stale marker could be interpreted as a new reconnect gap and overstate downtime.

## Repair

B13-C v0.3 must:

- clear legacy last_disconnect_ms during v0.1 migration;
- after accounting a reconnect gap, clear last_disconnect_ms once the new connection succeeds;
- preserve all raw-event semantics, frozen 12-symbol universe and alpha firewalls;
- retain the v0.2 heartbeat/process-restart ledger.

No protected liquidation outcome is inspected or reclassified by this repair.
