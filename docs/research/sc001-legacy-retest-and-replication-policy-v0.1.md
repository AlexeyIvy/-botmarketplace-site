# SC001 — Legacy Retest and Replication Policy v0.1

Date: 2026-09-17  
Status: **BINDING LEGACY GOVERNANCE AFTER STRATEGY-SELECTION RED-TEAM**

## 1. Purpose

The new strategy-selection framework does not imply that all prior strategies should be rerun on new data or new horizons.

Blanket retesting would:

- spend large compute/data budget on already falsified mechanisms;
- increase multiple-testing burden;
- invite outcome-driven rescue tuning;
- contaminate fresh chronologies without a clear scientific question.

Therefore prior SC001 families are classified by whether a new experiment would answer a genuinely new question.

## 2. General rule

A prior strategy may be revisited only under a **new experiment ID** and only when at least one of the following is true:

1. the old test was scope-limited in a way the new test prospectively and materially fixes;
2. the old result identified a predictive component that was never economically tested in the new role;
3. the new candidate changes the economic mechanism or eligible universe rather than merely the parameter neighborhood;
4. there is a clearly documented simulator/model limitation whose correction is necessary to distinguish strategy economics from model bias, and the old market economics were not already independently fatal.

Changing only threshold/lookback/hold/latency after a FAIL is not a valid reason.

## 3. E001 — hourly mean reversion

Classification: **NO DIRECT RETEST**.

Reason:

- terminal historical failure remains valid;
- hourly horizon is outside the expanded core scalp boundary of approximately <=30 minutes;
- no new framework component creates a specific reason to rerun the same hourly rule.

A new multi-minute H3/H4 mean-reversion candidate is allowed, but it is a new family with new ID, fresh causal rules and fresh evidence; it is not E001 rescue.

## 4. E002 — aggressive-flow / TFI microstructure signal

Classification: **NO STANDALONE RETEST; AUXILIARY REUSE ALLOWED UNDER NEW ID**.

Reason:

- predictive microstructure evidence existed;
- standalone taker economics failed;
- rerunning the same standalone rule is not justified.

TFI/order-flow may appear later only as a prospectively frozen ranking/veto/timing feature inside a different economic mechanism, with fresh evidence and an explicit A/B or incremental-value question.

## 5. E003 — rare flow-impulse continuation

Classification: **NO DIRECT RETEST**.

Reason:

- primary gross continuation mechanism failed;
- no identified scope limitation currently justifies a strict replication;
- new continuation research should target a genuinely different horizon/mechanism cell, not E003 parameter rescue.

## 6. E004 / E005 — compression breakout family

Classification: **NO DIRECT RETEST**.

Reason:

- E004 failed frozen discovery economics;
- E005 depended on a viable E004 prerequisite and remains closed.

A future H3/H4 multi-minute continuation/volatility-expansion strategy is allowed only as a new candidate with a new causal definition, fresh chronology and explicit distinction from E004.

## 7. E006 — same-venue spot/perp basis convergence

Classification: **STRICT MULTI-ASSET REPLICATION JUSTIFIED — NEW ID E006R1 CANDIDATE**.

Reason:

- the mechanism is economically orthogonal to the heavily tested same-asset reversal/continuation families;
- the original BTC study suffered from event scarcity / insufficient headroom evidence on a single instrument;
- the new framework specifically requires multi-asset breadth and cheap structural-feasibility screening before heavy engineering;
- a multi-asset strict replication directly tests whether the old scarcity was BTC-specific or structural.

Conditions:

- no reuse of old BTC profitability as promotional proof;
- no threshold/baseline/hold rescue based on old outcome;
- first run a cheap pair-eligibility / event-frequency / four-fill-cost feasibility card;
- only if structurally feasible may multi-leg engineering proceed;
- fresh chronology and holdouts required.

E006R1 is justified, but it is not automatically next if cheaper under-covered candidates dominate on information value and feasibility.

## 8. E007 / E007R1 — displacement reversal

Classification: **NO FURTHER DIRECT RETEST**.

Reason:

- E007 already received a strict multi-asset replication under E007R1;
- E007R1 produced adequate activity but failed frozen cross-market headroom/breadth/latency gates;
- another direct replication or threshold neighborhood would be rescue tuning.

A future H3 multi-minute mean-reversion candidate is allowed only if its signal construction, economic horizon and evidence are genuinely new and prospectively frozen.

## 9. E008 — passive maker / spread capture

Classification: **NO BTC SAME-RULE RETEST; NEW ELIGIBLE-MARKET MAKER FAMILY MAY BE STUDIED**.

Reason:

Two findings coexist:

1. BTC economics were genuinely poor: spread/gross edge was insufficient relative to regular-user fee floor;
2. the frozen queue model was also too adversarial to represent a central FIFO estimate.

Improving the queue simulator does not rescue poor BTC economics.

A future maker candidate is allowed only if a new prospectively selected market universe first passes a spread/fee/adverse-selection feasibility screen. That would be a new family/ID, not E008 rescue.

## 10. E009 — volatility-normalized displacement reversal

Classification: **NO DIRECT RETEST**.

Reason:

- adequate sample and all 8/8 active markets;
- terminal fresh Discovery failure on cross-market average, breadth, robust pooled headroom and latency robustness;
- concentration was not the failure mode.

No E009R2 threshold/lookback/MAD/target variant is authorized from the same evidence.

## 12. Binding principle

The new research framework improves **future experiment selection and validation**. It does not erase old evidence.

Retest only when the new experiment answers a genuinely different scientific question; otherwise preserve the failure and spend research budget elsewhere.
