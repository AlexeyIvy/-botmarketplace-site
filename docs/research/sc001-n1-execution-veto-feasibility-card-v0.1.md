## 2. Economic mechanism

Economic thesis:

A weak microstructure signal need not fund its own round trip if it prevents an expensive bad entry or changes execution aggressiveness on a trade that already exists.

Candidate auxiliary blocks:

- RB005 extreme aggressive-flow exhaustion / anti-chase;
- RB016 one-sided liquidity-vacuum onset;
- RB017 quoted-spread state.

Who pays for edge:

Avoided adverse selection / avoided late aggressive entry rather than forecast profit generated by a new trade.

Main falsification:

If BASE vs BASE+VETO does not improve net execution/risk per original opportunity after accounting for skipped opportunity value, N1 adds no economic value.
