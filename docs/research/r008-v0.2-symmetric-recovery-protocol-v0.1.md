## 3. Economic hypothesis

### H-R008-v0.2 — temporary crisis risk, automatic dry-powder recovery

The beneficial part of v0.1 may come from temporarily owning more BTC while market stress is severe, not from holding the additional exposure until a new all-time high.

A cleaner barbell should therefore:

- own a small permanent BTC sleeve;
- increase BTC exposure only while drawdown severity warrants it;
- reduce that extra exposure as the drawdown itself recovers;
- rebuild cash before a full ATH recovery is required;
- use no extra prediction signal.

### Falsification

The v0.2 mechanism is weakened or rejected if:

- it remains economically dominated by STATIC15;
- recovery release destroys the previously observed crisis Benefit10;
- repeated threshold crossing creates enough turnover that moderate costs erase the advantage;
- it still spends at least half of primary history at 20% BTC;
- it fails to improve dry-powder availability relative to v0.1;
- pre-2020 and post-2020 behavior point in materially different directions.

## 5. Drawdown state

Using the daily reference price:

`Peak_t = max(price_0 ... price_t)`

`DD_t = price_t / Peak_t - 1`

State is computed only from information available at observation `t`.

The target determined from `DD_t` applies to return `t+1`.

No same-observation execution and no future information.

---

## 6. Exact symmetric target rule

R008 v0.2 has **no sticky tranche memory**.

Target BTC exposure is a deterministic function of the **current drawdown only**:

| Current closing drawdown | BTC target |
|---|---:|
| `DD > -20%` | 10.0% |
| `-35% < DD <= -20%` | 12.5% |
| `-50% < DD <= -35%` | 15.0% |
| `-65% < DD <= -50%` | 17.5% |
| `DD <= -65%` | 20.0% |

This same mapping governs both directions.

Examples:

- 12% drawdown -> 10%;
- 27% drawdown -> 12.5%;
- 42% drawdown -> 15%;
- 58% drawdown -> 17.5%;
- 70% drawdown -> 20%;
- recovery from -70% to -58% -> 17.5%;
- recovery to -47% -> 15%;
- recovery to -31% -> 12.5%;
- recovery to -18% -> 10%.

If price later re-crosses a threshold in the adverse direction, the corresponding tranche is re-deployed. If it recovers back through that threshold, it is released again.

This repeated crossing is intentional in v0.2. There is **no hysteresis** and no cooldown. Boundary churn is measured as an outcome rather than pre-solved with another parameter.

A new ATH simply implies `DD = 0` and therefore a 10% target. No special reset action is needed beyond the deterministic mapping.

11. REPLAY_2020 and POST_2023 do not both contradict the pre-2020 direction.

Assign REDESIGN if meaningful crisis benefit remains but one or more central architecture goals fail, especially:

- STATIC15 still dominates;
- 20% occupancy remains >=50%;

- boundary churn creates meaningful cost fragility;
- historical behavior is mixed enough that forward promotion is unjustified.

- it fails to beat STATIC10 on geometric growth in both major historical regimes;
- deep-crisis Benefit10 becomes non-positive;
- costs erase the mechanism;
- it is clearly worse than v0.1 without solving the trapped-exposure problem;
