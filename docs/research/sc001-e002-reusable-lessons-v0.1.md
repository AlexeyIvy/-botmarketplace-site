## 1. Core classification

E002 should be retained as:

**confirmed predictive microstructure feature; rejected standalone taker strategy.**

The aggressive trade-flow imbalance signal showed repeated positive short-horizon predictive ordering across:

- Binance DEV discovery;
- Binance chronological DEV confirmation;
- OKX same-venue transaction-price replication;
- OKX causal L2 midquote confirmation.

The executable Q1 taker-economics layer then showed that this predictive information is far too small to overcome the frozen regular-user taker fee burden as a standalone 5-second round-trip strategy.

## 2. What failed economically

Primary and stress diagnostics showed:

- positive gross midquote edge remained present;
- positive pre-fee executable edge remained present;
- visible-book completion was effectively not the limiting factor in the shown primary/stress scenarios;
- funding cost was negligible;
- spread/depth consumed only a small fraction of the already-small gross edge;
- the dominant failure mechanism was the approximately 10 bps round-trip taker fee versus only a few hundredths of a bp of pre-fee executable edge.

Therefore E002 was not a near miss. Fee scale exceeded the monetizable standalone edge by roughly two orders of magnitude.

## 3. Reusable role for the signal

Do not treat TFI as an independent BUY/SELL engine in future work by default.

Potentially useful future roles are:

1. **auxiliary filter / veto** for a stronger base strategy;
2. **confidence or ranking feature** among trades already generated by another mechanism;
3. **execution-timing / adverse-selection feature** for orders that would occur anyway;
4. **ensemble / meta-model feature**, where the test is incremental value versus a base model, not standalone profitability.

The important economic distinction is that an auxiliary feature can improve or suppress an already-planned trade without necessarily creating an additional fee-paying round trip.

## 4. Signal interpretation

E002 is better understood as a return-ranking / conditional-magnitude feature than as a high-hit-rate binary directional classifier.

Future use should therefore prefer continuous or rank-based conditioning and explicitly test **incremental predictive/economic value** over a base strategy.

## 5. Capacity and execution lessons

The Q1 executable study showed that, for the tested BTC-USDT-SWAP scenarios, visible 400-level depth was ample at the primary 10k USDT target. Even severe 50% depth haircut remained fully completable in the observed postmortem output.

Execution-state waits were also small in the observed postmortem output (single-digit millisecond median and only tens of milliseconds at p95/p99), so book-update availability was not the binding failure mechanism.

This suggests future BTC scalp research at similar order sizes should apply an **economics hurdle early**, rather than assuming depth engineering is the first-order problem.

## 6. Threshold-tie finding

Read-only postmortem diagnostics on completed trades showed very high equality between `abs(TFI)` and the rolling percentile threshold:

- q90: about 59.7% of deduplicated completed trades tied the threshold;
- q95: about 85.0%;
- q97.5: about 95.3%.

These diagnostics are on deduplicated completed trades only, not all raw candidates, so they are descriptive rather than a new inferential test.

Still, the result strongly suggests a highly discrete / mass-point-heavy score distribution. Consequently, a nearest-rank percentile trigger with `>= threshold` is not very selective at the upper percentiles.

This is a key methodological lesson for future candidates: bounded/discrete microstructure ratios should not automatically be assumed to produce clean percentile tails. Predeclared tie handling, continuous ranking, or a different signal representation may be needed in a *new* experiment. None of those changes may retroactively alter E002.

## 7. Economics-first lesson for E003+

Before spending heavy L2 compute on a new candidate, require a predeclared coarse economics hurdle:

- expected gross move must be of a scale plausibly able to clear fee + spread/depth costs;
- turnover should be constrained explicitly;
- signals that create thousands of short round trips with sub-bp gross movement should be deprioritized unless they are intended as auxiliary features rather than standalone triggers.
